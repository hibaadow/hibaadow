# Is a data protection officer required? (Article 37)

A DPO is required where (a) processing is by a public authority, (b) core activities consist of
processing that requires regular and systematic monitoring of data subjects on a large scale, or
(c) core activities consist of large-scale processing of special category or criminal-offence data.
