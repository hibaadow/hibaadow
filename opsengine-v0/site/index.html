How it works

1 · Find

See every gap

We check your people, documents and equipment against what each of your main contractors requires.

2 · Fix

We chase it

We get new card photos from workers, certificates from your broker, and quotes and bookings from training providers.

3 · Check

Proof, checked

Nothing is closed until the card or certificate is in and checked. Anything unclear goes to a person.

4 · Keep

Stay ready

We remind people before things expire. You get a weekly summary and a record you can show any main contractor.

You stay in control

Your OK first

Nothing that costs money or commits you happens without your approval. One tap from an email.

Qualified sign-off

Safety Statements and RAMS are reviewed and signed off by a qualified safety professional, never by software.
