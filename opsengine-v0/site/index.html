Common questions

What does it cost?

The pilot is free for 30 days. After that it's a fixed monthly fee based on the size of your team, agreed with you before the pilot ends. No contract: you can stop any time.

Do my workers need to download an app?

No. We message them on their phone when something is due, and they reply with a photo of their card. That's it.

Who signs off our Safety Statement and RAMS?

A qualified safety professional reviews and signs off every safety document before it's released. Software never signs anything off.

Does this replace our safety consultant or PSCS?

We handle all the admin for you: chasing, checking and keeping the records. What we don't replace are the roles the law says a qualified person must hold, like your PSCS, and the inspections and training themselves. We organise those for you and keep the proof.

Will you spend money or book things without asking?

Never. Anything that costs money or commits you, like a course booking, waits for your OK, which you can give with one tap from an email.

What if a photo of a card is unclear?

Nothing is guessed. If a card or certificate can't be read with confidence, a person checks it, and if it's still unclear we ask for a new photo.

Where is our data kept, and who sees it?

In the EU. Only you and our team see your company's records, and worker details are used only for their site paperwork. If you leave, we give you a copy and delete the rest.

What do you need from us to get started?

Phone photos are fine: your Safety Statement, insurance certificates, a list of your workers and sites, and the requirements your main contractors gave you. We'll chase anything that's missing.
