# Pricing (internal working plan, not public yet)

Not on the website or in first messages until it's been tested on real conversations.
Use it on calls, after asking question 5 ("what would that be worth a month?").

| Plan | Team size | Monthly (ex VAT) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | up to 10 workers | €299 |
| Standard | 11-25 workers | €499 |
| Plus | 26-50 workers | €799 |

- Annual: 2 months free. No contract, cancel any time.
- Founding customers (first 3 pilot firms who continue): 25% off for the first year.
- Extra: safety document reviews (Safety Statement / RAMS) at the reviewer's fee + a small margin.
- Training, inspections, courses: the customer pays the provider directly.

## How to test it
- Ask the worth question before saying any number; write down their answer.
- Most say €500+: prices too low. Most say under €200: go after bigger firms, or offer a lighter
  "check + remind" plan (no chasing).
- Agree the price in week 1-2 of the 30-day pilot (at the first feedback call).
- Publish prices on the website once 2 of the first 3 pilot firms pay.
