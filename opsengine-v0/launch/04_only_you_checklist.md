# Only-you checklist

Things that legally or practically need the founder. Claude prepares everything around them.
Do them **in this order**, and only when the daily plan says so. Fees and rules change: check
the current official page when you do each one (Claude will look it up with you).

## Before the first pilot (free pilots can start without most of this)
- [ ] **Pick a working name and email.** Claude suggests names; you choose one; set up a free
      email for outreach. ~15 min.
- [ ] **Find the safety partner** (see `02_outreach_safety_consultants.md`). No safety document
      goes to a customer without their review.
- [ ] **Simple pilot agreement.** Claude drafts it (what we do, what we don't, data handling,
      the customer approves spending). Ideally a solicitor glances at it before real data arrives.

## Before the first paid customer
- [ ] **Register the business.** Easiest start: register as a **sole trader** with Revenue.
      A limited company (CRO) can come later; an accountant can advise which suits you. ~1 hour.
- [ ] **Business bank account.** Any Irish bank or a fintech business account. ~30 min.
- [ ] **Payments.** Stripe, or invoices paid by bank transfer to start. ~30 min, needs your ID.
- [ ] **Insurance.** Professional indemnity insurance before handling real customer
      compliance work. Ask a broker for a quote; Claude writes the email. ~20 min.
- [ ] **Data protection (GDPR).** Customer worker data is personal data. Claude drafts the
      privacy notice and data processing terms; real data stays out of the git repo (already set up).

## Never delegate
- Saying yes to a customer and agreeing a price.
- Anything that commits money.
- Being the accountable person if something goes wrong.
