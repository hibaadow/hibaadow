# Phone cheat sheet and follow-ups

**Wording rule (founder):** never write "no sales pitch" or similar in outreach; it puts people off.

## Phone calls (M&N Civils, BH Groundworks)
Goal of the call: **get the right person's email**. Not to sell. 1 minute.

> "Hi, I'm Hiba from ClearToWork. Who looks after your Safe Pass, CSCS and insurance paperwork?
> I'd like to send them a short email. ... Great, what's the best email for them?"

Then: "Thanks, I'll send it today." Tell Claude the email; Claude sends message 4 or 5.

### If they ask...
| They say | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "What is it?" | "We take the site paperwork off subcontractors: we chase workers for card photos, keep insurance certs in date, and have the pack ready for each main contractor." |
| "How much?" | "We're running a free 30-day pilot with 3 firms right now, so nothing. After that it depends on team size; we'd agree it together." (never quote prices on a first call) |
| "We have someone for that." | "Perfect, they're who I'd love to talk to. It's about saving them time, not replacing them." |
| "Is our data safe?" | "Stored in the EU, only used for your paperwork, and you can leave any time and we delete it." |
| "Are you a safety consultant?" | "No. We handle the admin. Anything that needs a qualified safety person, like signing off RAMS, is done by one." |
| "Send me something." | "Will do: what's the email?" (then Claude sends the message + website link) |
| "Not interested." | "No problem at all, thanks for your time." (mark them as no; never contact again) |

## Follow-ups (send 5 working days after the first message, only if no reply)
One follow-up only. Same email thread (reply to your own sent email).
