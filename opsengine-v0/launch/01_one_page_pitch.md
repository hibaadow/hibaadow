# OpsEngine: your compliance paperwork, handled

**For:** Irish construction subcontractors (5-50 workers) who work for main contractors.

## The problem
Every main contractor wants a different pile of paperwork before your crew can go on site:
Safe Pass and CSCS cards, a current Safety Statement, RAMS for each job, insurance certificates
at their minimums, inductions, equipment inspections. It changes per contractor, cards expire,
and chasing it eats evenings. One expired card can keep a worker off site, and a failed
prequalification can lose you a job.

## What we do
We take the compliance admin off your plate:

1. **Find the gaps.** We check your people, documents and equipment against what each of your
   main contractors requires, and show you exactly what's missing or about to expire.
2. **Fix them.** We chase workers for photos of new cards, request certificates from your broker,
   compare training providers and line up bookings, and prepare documents for review.
3. **Check the proof.** Every card and certificate is checked before an issue is closed.
4. **Keep you ready.** Renewals are tracked, reminders go out before things expire, and you get
   a short weekly summary plus a full record you can show any main contractor or inspector.

**You stay in control.** Nothing that costs money or commits you happens without your OK.
Safety Statements and RAMS are reviewed and signed off by a qualified safety professional,
not by software.

## Why us
Built on our own compliance engine: it tracks every requirement as a rule, works through a fix
for each problem, follows up on its own and keeps a tamper-evident record of everything done.
That means faster, cheaper and more thorough than doing it by hand.
