# Onboarding email (send after the pilot is agreed)

**Subject:** Getting you set up with ClearToWork

Hi [first name],

Great to have you on board. To get your first compliance check done, could you send us the
following? Photos from your phone are perfect, no need to scan anything. Send whatever you have
and we'll chase the rest.

**Your company**
1. Your current Safety Statement (any format).
2. Insurance certificates: employer's liability, public liability (and professional indemnity if you have it).
3. Your tax clearance (a screenshot of the access number is fine).

**Your sites and main contractors**
4. The sites you're on now, and which main contractor runs each one.
5. Any prequalification forms or requirement lists those main contractors gave you.

**Your team**
6. A list of your workers: name, role, mobile number and preferred language.
7. A photo of each worker's Safe Pass and CSCS cards (front is usually enough).

**If you use subcontractors**
8. A contact email for each one.

**Plant and equipment you bring to site**
9. A list of items with their last inspection date (a photo of your register is fine).

Reply to this email with whatever you have. Within two working days we'll send your first
compliance check showing what's in date, what's missing, and what we're already fixing.

Thanks,
[Your name]
ClearToWork · [phone]

*Your workers' details are only used to manage their site paperwork, stored in the EU, and
deleted when you ask us to. We'll send you a short notice you can share with your team.*
