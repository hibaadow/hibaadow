# Pilot agreement (draft for a solicitor to check)

Plain-English draft for the free 30-day pilot. Not legal advice: have a solicitor read it before
the first customer signs. Placeholders are in [brackets].

---

**ClearToWork pilot agreement**

Between **[Your name], trading as ClearToWork**, [address] ("ClearToWork", "we")
and **[Customer company name]**, [address] ("you").

**1. What we'll do (the pilot)**
For 30 days from [start date] we will:
- check your workers, documents and equipment against the requirements of the main contractors
  you name, and show you what's missing or about to expire;
- chase workers, your insurance broker and providers for missing cards, certificates and
  documents, and check them when they arrive;
- compare training and inspection providers and prepare bookings for your approval;
- prepare draft Safety Statements and RAMS for review by a qualified safety professional;
- send you a short weekly summary and keep a full record of everything done.

**2. What we won't do**
- We don't certify compliance, and we don't take on any legal duty-holder role (for example
  PSCS/PSDP). Certificates come from the qualified people named in each record.
- We don't carry out physical inspections, training or repairs; qualified providers do.
- We don't spend your money or commit you to anything without your approval.
- Safety documents are never released without sign-off by a qualified safety professional.

**3. Your part**
- Give us accurate information and tell us when something changes (new workers, sites, contractors).
- Tell your workers that ClearToWork handles their compliance paperwork (we provide a short notice).
- Approve or decline requests within a reasonable time.
- About 15 minutes of feedback every two weeks.

**4. Price**
The pilot is free. Before it ends we'll agree a monthly price with you in writing; there's no
obligation to continue.

**5. Your data**
- You own your data. We process it only to provide this service, under the data processing terms
  in Schedule 1 (`08_gdpr_pack.md`, section 3).
- At the end, if you don't continue, we give you a copy of your records and delete them within 30 days.

**6. Responsibility**
- We'll do the work with reasonable care and skill. You remain responsible for your own legal duties
  under health and safety law.
- [Limit of liability: solicitor to advise, typically capped and excluding indirect loss.]

**7. Ending the pilot**
Either side can end the pilot at any time by email. Section 5 still applies after it ends.

**8. Law**
This agreement is governed by the law of Ireland.
