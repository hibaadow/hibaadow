# Finding the qualified safety partner

We need a qualified health and safety consultant in Ireland to review and sign off Safety
Statements, RAMS and high-risk corrective actions. Best case: a freelancer who wants steady
paid review work, or a co-founder who also knows subcontractors.

**Where to find them:** LinkedIn search "health and safety consultant Ireland construction",
"safety officer Dublin"; the IOSH Ireland branch; local H&S consultancies' websites.

## Message 1: LinkedIn connection note (under 300 characters)
> Hi [name], I'm building a compliance admin service for Irish subcontractors (chasing cards,
> certs and paperwork). I need a qualified H&S pro to review safety documents, paid per review.
> Open to a quick chat?

## Message 2: after they connect
> Thanks for connecting, [name]. Short version: subcontractors lose hours chasing Safe Pass
> photos, insurance certs and main contractor paperwork. We handle that admin for them. Our
> engine finds the gaps, chases people and keeps the record; it never signs anything off.
>
> That's where I'd need you: reviewing drafted Safety Statements and RAMS before release, and
> signing off high-risk corrective actions. Paid per review, flexible, remote, a few hours a
> month to start, more as we grow. Also happy to talk about a bigger role if you know the
> subcontractor world well.
>
> Would a 15-minute call this week work?

## Message 3: follow-up after 4 days, no reply
> Hi [name], just bumping this in case it got buried. Even a "not for me, but try X" would help!

## On the call (15 minutes)
1. What does a typical subcontractor get wrong on paperwork?
2. How long does a Safety Statement / RAMS review take you? What would you charge per review?
3. Would you be comfortable being the named reviewer for a small number of firms?
4. Do you know subcontractors who'd try a free pilot?
5. Interested in a bigger role (shares, revenue share) if this works?
