Your crew, clear to work.
The paperwork, handled.

Every main contractor wants a different pile of documents before your crew can go on site: Safe Pass and CSCS cards, a current Safety Statement, RAMS for each job, insurance at their minimums. It changes per contractor, cards expire, and chasing it eats your evenings. ClearToWork takes that admin off your plate.

How it works

1 · Find

We check your people, documents and equipment against what each of your main contractors requires, and show you every gap.

2 · Fix

We chase it: new card photos from workers, certificates from your broker, quotes and bookings from training providers.

3 · Check

Nothing is closed until the card or certificate is in and checked. Anything unclear goes to a person.

4 · Keep

Reminders before things expire, a weekly summary, and a ready-to-send pack for each main contractor.

You stay in control

  • Nothing that costs money or commits you happens without your OK, one tap from an email.
  • Safety Statements and RAMS are signed off by a qualified safety professional, never by software.
  • Your workers don't need an app: they just reply with a photo of their card.

Your data

  • Stored in the EU, used only for your site paperwork.
  • A full record of everything done, ready for any audit.
  • Leave any time: you get a copy, we delete the rest.
