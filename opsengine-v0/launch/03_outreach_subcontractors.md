# Talking to subcontractors (the 10 conversations)

**Goal of weeks 1-2:** 10 conversations, not sales. Learn what hurts. If 3 or more say "I'd pay
for that", offer the pilot.

**Who:** owners or office managers of groundworks, drylining, scaffolding, electrical, M&E,
formwork and roofing firms with 5-50 workers, ideally in Dublin / Leinster.
**Where:** LinkedIn ("owner" + trade + "Dublin"), Google Maps for the trade + area (call or email
the number on their website), local trade Facebook groups, people you know who work on sites.

## LinkedIn / email (first message)
> Hi [name], quick question as a [trade] firm owner: how much time does your team spend
> chasing Safe Pass photos, insurance certs and main contractor paperwork? I'm building a
> service that handles that admin for subcontractors and want to get it right. Could I ask
> you 3 quick questions? 10 minutes, no sales pitch.

## WhatsApp / text (if they gave you their number)
> Hi [name], [your name] here. I'm talking to [trade] firms about the paperwork main
> contractors ask for (cards, certs, RAMS). Got 10 minutes this week for 3 questions?
> Not selling anything yet, just want to learn.

## Follow-up after 3 days
> Hi [name], no worries if you're flat out. If easier, just reply with your biggest
> paperwork headache in one line and I'll leave you be!

## The 10-minute call (write down their exact words)
1. Walk me through the last time a main contractor asked for documents. What happened?
2. Who in your firm deals with it, and how many hours a week does it take?
3. Has anyone ever been kept off site, or have you lost a job, because of paperwork?
4. What do you use today (spreadsheet, folders, an app, a consultant)? What does it cost?
5. If someone handled all of it for you, what would that be worth a month?

**If they're keen:** "We're taking 3 firms on a free 30-day pilot. Want to be one?"
Send them `01_one_page_pitch.md` as a PDF afterwards.
