Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains, in plain English, what happens to the details you share when you join the Sway waitlist.

Who we are

Sway is a friendship app for girls, based in Ireland. We're responsible for your information (the "data controller"). You can contact us any time at hello@example.com.

What we collect

  • Your first name, email and city
  • Your age range and the plans you'd like to do (if you tell us)
  • Your TikTok or Instagram handle (if you tell us)
  • When you signed up, and that you agreed to hear from us

Why we collect it

Only to email you about Sway's launch, and to understand which cities to open in first. We use your details because you gave us permission (your consent) by ticking the box on the form.

Who can see it

Only the Sway team. We never sell or share your details. They're stored securely with Supabase (our database, hosted in the EU) and the site runs on Vercel. These services only store and process the information for us.

How long we keep it

Until Sway launches in your city, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. If Sway doesn't go ahead, we'll delete the waitlist.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or stop hearing from us, at any time. Just email us or tap "unsubscribe" in any email we send. You can also complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission.

Cookies

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. The form remembers what you've typed on your own phone, so you don't lose it if you close the page. That never leaves your device until you tap "Join the waitlist".
